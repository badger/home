# Critter Quest – Sunshine Edition

An original Game Boy-style monster-catching RPG where your partner critter follows you around.

## Usage

- **D-pad**: walk (tap to turn on the spot)
- **SELECT**: talk, read signs, confirm
- **BACK**: cancel or close a menu
- **MENU**: open the start menu (Team, Bag, Save)

Walk through tall grass to meet wild critters. In battle, choose **FIGHT**,
**TEAM**, **BAG** or **RUN**. Throw orbs at weakened critters to catch them,
and visit the clinic or your mom's house to heal. Progress is saved to the
badge when you save from the menu or leave the app.

All characters, creatures and artwork are original.

## Requirements

No network or extra hardware needed.
