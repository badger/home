# Contacts

Exchange contact cards with other badges.

## Usage

Set your contact name in Settings, then use the direction controls and Select to share and browse cards with a nearby badge over the badge link.

Press Right on the home screen to show QR codes for your own GitHub and LinkedIn links, so anyone can scan them with a phone. Press Right again to switch links, and Left or Back to return.

Your card shows the GitHub avatar cached by the Badge app, so open Badge once with Wi-Fi and your GitHub username set. Until then, it shows your badge's identicon. A swap shares only your name, GitHub username and LinkedIn link; other badges never receive your picture.
