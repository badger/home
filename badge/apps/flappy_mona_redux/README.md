# Flappy Mona Redux

A full-resolution remake of Flappy Mona where fish make Mona immortal for 5 seconds and floating brick towers with stairs she can climb keep turning up between the walls.

## Usage

Press Select to start. In the air, Select flaps and steers Mona through the gaps in the walls. Once she is running on a tower, Select makes her jump. Your best score is saved on the badge. No extra hardware or network access is required.

## What is new

- The game runs at the badge's full 320x240 resolution, with redrawn artwork.
- Fish drift through the gaps of some walls and float above the towers. Grab one and Mona turns immortal for 5 seconds: she glows gold, flies straight through walls and can rest on the ground.
- The timer in the top right counts down the immortality, and the glow flickers for the last two seconds. Grabbing another fish restarts the 5 seconds.
- Walls Mona passes while immortal still count toward your score.
- The game opens with a big roof to climb, and after that a stair tower arrives every 3 to 5 walls.

## The towers

Fly into the stairs on the left of a tower and Mona climbs them on her own, then runs along the top. The camera rises with her and glides back down once she leaves the tower. She steps down the stairs on the right and drops back into flight.

| Tower | Stairs up | Top | Stairs down | On top |
|-------|-----------|-----|-------------|--------|
| Steps | 4 | short | 4 | a fish to jump for |
| Hill | 6 | medium | 6 | a spike and a fish |
| Roof | 8 | long | 4 | 2 spikes, 2 rolling drums and 2 fish |

The towers come in the order roof, steps, hill, steps and then repeat.

- Touching a spike ends the run. A rolling drum that reaches Mona flattens her. Jump to clear each hazard: the timing window is about a third of a second.
- Fish float above the hazards, so a good jump also earns 5 seconds of immortality. While she glows, Mona passes straight through the hazards.
- Every tower is optional. A low tunnel, about 60 pixels high, runs underneath for pilots who prefer to fly, but the brick and the spikes on its underside are deadly.
- Clearing a tower scores 1 point. Immortality that runs out while Mona is inside the brick or overlapping a hazard ends the run.

## Tuning

- Fish frequency is set by `FIRST_FISH`, `FISH_EVERY` and `FISH_SPACING` in `course.py`, and the immortality length by `IMMORTAL_SECONDS` in `mona.py`.
- `TOWER_ORDER` and `TOWER_EVERY` in `course.py` choose which towers appear and how many plain walls separate them. `TOWER_CLEARANCE` sets the empty space before and after each tower.
- Each tower is a `Layout(up, deck_cols, down, ...)` in `LAYOUTS` in `tower.py`. `up` and `down` count the stairs, `deck_cols` sets the length of the top, and the `spikes`, `rollers` and `fish` lists place things along it. `FIRST_TREAD_Y` and `UNDERSIDE_Y` set the height of the tunnel.

The artwork is generated by `tools/build_flappy_mona_redux_assets.py` from the original Flappy Mona sprites. The original game is still available as Flappy Mona.
