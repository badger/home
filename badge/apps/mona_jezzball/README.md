# Mona Jezzball

Claim cozy corners by carving the playfield while keeping Mona and her friends safe.

## Usage

Move the cursor with the arrows, press Select to start a cut, and press Back to switch between horizontal and vertical cuts; the slicer marker shows the selected direction. The cursor can move anywhere, and a cut started in claimed territory travels through it into open space; once in open space, each end stops independently at the next wall, claimed area, or edge. Claim at least 75% of the field before losing all three paws. Mona's animated sprite always faces left or right, following her horizontal movement. Clear a level to meet more Monas, up to five, and face faster movement. Press Select to begin, continue, or replay. No extra hardware or network access is required.
