# Badge

Display your GitHub profile and contribution graph.

## Requirements

This app needs internet access. Add your Wi-Fi details and your GitHub username (`GITHUB_USERNAME`, optionally `GITHUB_TOKEN`) to `secrets.py` on the badge before using it, or enter them with the Settings app.

You can also set `CONTACT_NAME`, `CONTACT_TITLE`, `CONTACT_COMPANY` and `CONTACT_LINKEDIN` in Settings. Your job title and company appear under your name.

## Usage

Launch the app and wait for your profile and contribution graph to download.
