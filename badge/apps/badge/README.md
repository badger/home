# Badge

Display your GitHub profile and contribution graph.

## Requirements

This app needs internet access. Add your Wi-Fi details and your GitHub username (`GITHUB_USERNAME`, optionally `GITHUB_TOKEN`) to `secrets.py` on the badge before using it, or enter them with the Settings app.

## Usage

Launch the app and wait for your profile and contribution graph to download.

- The stats view initially shows Followers, Contribs and Repos. Press **Down** to scroll one row at a time to Best Day (most contributions in a single day over the last year) and Yrs on GH. Press **Up** to scroll back. Arrows at the right show when more stats are available; scrolling stops at either end.
- Press **Left** or **Right** to cycle between three views: stats; your `@username` across the whole screen with your full name below it; and a profile view with a smaller avatar beside your full name and company, and your `@username` large underneath. Returning to stats preserves your scroll position.
- Press **Select** to animate your avatar with a randomly chosen coin flip, clockwise spin, or three shrink-and-grow pulses.
- Hold **Left** and **Right** together to refresh your profile, stats and avatar.
