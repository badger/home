# Badge

Display your GitHub profile and contribution graph.

## Requirements

This app needs internet access. Add your Wi-Fi details and your GitHub username (`GITHUB_USERNAME`, optionally `GITHUB_TOKEN`) to `secrets.py` on the badge before using it, or enter them with the Settings app.

## Usage

Launch the app and wait for your profile and contribution graph to download.

- The stats view shows, top to bottom, your followers, your best day (most contributions in a single day over the last year), and your years on GitHub ("Yrs on GH").
- Press **Up** or **Down** to cycle between three views: stats; your `@username` across the whole screen with your full name below it; and a profile view with a smaller avatar beside your full name and company, and your `@username` large underneath.
- Press **Select** to animate your avatar with a randomly chosen coin flip, clockwise spin, or three shrink-and-grow pulses.
- Hold **Left** and **Right** together to refresh your profile, stats and avatar.
